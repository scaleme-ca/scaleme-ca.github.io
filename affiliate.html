Tracking, cost, and how long it takes.

How does a sale actually get tracked?

Each partner gets a link carrying their own tag. When somebody clicks it, that tag is stored on their device for an agreed window, and when they buy, the platform is told what happened by a message sent server to server. That last part matters, because tracking that relies only on the browser now misses a meaningful share of sales.

What does the platform cost to run?

Depends on the platform and your volume, and it usually lands somewhere between thirty and a few hundred dollars a month. Some ecommerce and subscription tools include it. That cost goes on your own account directly, and we will tell you the expected figure before anything gets built.

How long before a program produces revenue?

Setup takes two to three weeks. First sales usually arrive within a month of the first partners going live, since a partner with an audience can send traffic immediately. Building it into a channel worth managing takes about six months of steady recruitment.

Do you work with companies outside Canada?

Yes. Nothing here needs anyone in the room, and we work across time zones as a matter of course. Payout rules and disclosure requirements differ by country, so we check what applies where your partners actually are.

What commission should we pay?

It depends entirely on your margin and whether the sale repeats. Subscription products can afford to pay more because the customer keeps paying, and one off physical products usually cannot. We bring you two or three options with the maths on each, and you pick.

What stops a partner sending fake sales?

Anything that looks like a self referral, a repeat from the same card, or a signup that never activates gets held before the payout run instead of after it. The terms let you claw back commission on refunded or fraudulent orders, which is much easier to enforce when it was written down before they joined.
