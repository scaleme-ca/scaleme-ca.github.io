Questions people ask before booking.

If yours is not here, email it over and we will answer it properly.

Who writes the copy?

We do, and it is included in the price. You talk us through the business and your customers on the first call, we write the pages, and you change anything that does not sound like you. If you would rather write it yourself, send your drafts and we will edit them and build around them.

Can you work on a site built in WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, or Shopify?

Yes, any of those, and custom builds too. We do not need to have built a site to fix it or take over looking after it. Send us the login and we will come back with what we would change and what it would cost.

What happens to my current site while you build the new one?

It stays live throughout. The new site is built on a private address that only you and we can reach, so nothing changes for your customers until you have signed it off. On launch day the domain gets pointed across and redirects are set up so your existing rankings carry over. The switch itself takes about an hour.

Our photos aren't great.

Most people's aren't. We work with what you have and use stock where it genuinely fits. Where the site needs original photography, we will either tell you what to shoot and how, or bring in a photographer and quote that separately.

Do you do logos and branding?

Yes, though it sits under our marketing service. If you already have a brand, refining how it is used across the site is part of the build: type, colour, spacing, and tone. Building an identity from scratch is quoted separately.

What if something breaks after launch?

Anything that shipped broken is fixed at no charge for the first thirty days. After that a care plan covers updates, backups, monitoring, and small changes, and one off fixes are quoted before we start them.

Can we start with a single page?

Yes. A landing page is a common first job, particularly if you are about to run ads, and it is an inexpensive way to see how we work before committing to a full site.

How will I know whether it is working?

Analytics go in before launch, with tracking on every form and phone number, so you can see how many enquiries arrived and where each one came from. On a care plan we send a written summary each month.
