Ownership, timing, and who does the writing.

Do you work with companies outside Canada?

Yes. Almost nothing on this page needs anyone in the room, and we work across time zones as a matter of course. Calls get scheduled around your working day.

Who writes the articles and the emails?

We do. Before anything gets written we use the product, read your support inbox, and go through the questions your sales calls keep raising. You review the first two pieces closely, and after that the voice usually holds without much correction.

We already have a brand. Can you work with it?

Yes, and often that is the better job. We keep what is recognisable and fix what is causing problems: versions that do not work small, colours that fail contrast, missing file formats, no guide for anyone else to follow.

How long before any of this shows results?

Email and social move fastest, because you already have the audience. Technical fixes can shift things within a few weeks. Articles usually take three to six months to pull meaningful search traffic, since that is how long indexing and ranking take on anything competitive. We will give you a realistic window for your situation before you commit to anything.
